Interactive 3D Building Configurator with Mobile AR

I built a browser-based configurator that lets users change a steel building’s dimensions, roof, and openings and see the 3D model update. Users can export models and share a conceptual design for mobile augmented reality review. The app uses one building definition for the editor and export.
