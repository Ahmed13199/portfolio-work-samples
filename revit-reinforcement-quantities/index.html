Revit Add-In for Reinforcement Quantities and Reporting

I built a Revit add-in that calculates reinforcement quantities for concrete elements. Engineers review mapped model inputs, identify elements that need attention, and see net tonnage separately from tonnage with allowance. The add-in generates Excel and PDF bills of quantities for review and handoff.
